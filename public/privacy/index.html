HamsterPi — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Welcome to HamsterPi ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in all data handling across our Web3 mobile gaming ecosystem within the Pi Network.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with HamsterPi via the Pi Browser or standard web browsers, we may collect the following limited information:

  • Pi Network Profile Data: Your public Pi username and unique Pioneer User ID (`uid`) provided through the official Pi SDK upon authentication.
  • Game Progression & Statistics: Game points, tap counts, mini-game high scores, upgrade levels, energy levels, and login streak records.
  • Transaction & Payment Logs: Pi payment identifiers (`paymentId`), transaction hashes (`txid`), purchased virtual items, and timestamps verified via the Pi Platform API.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

We believe in strict data minimization and user privacy:

  • We never ask for or store your Pi Wallet Passphrase, 24-word Secret Seed, or private keys.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal data such as government IDs, phone contacts, physical addresses, or credit cards.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your Pioneer account securely using Pi Network's official OAuth mechanism.
  • To save your gameplay progression, upgrades, and leaderboard standings across sessions.
  • To verify and deliver in-game virtual rewards purchased through official Pi payments.
  • To display rewarded video advertisements via the official Pi Ad Network in compliance with Pi Developer policies.

4. In-Game Points & Cryptocurrency Notice

Internal HamsterPi game points, coins, and golden seeds are strictly entertainment gaming assets. They represent in-app achievements, prestige, and virtual progression, and are not convertible to fiat or external cryptocurrency.

5. Data Retention & Security

Your game records are stored using industry-standard encrypted Supabase PostgreSQL cloud infrastructure with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. Payment states are auditable and strictly validated on our secure backend server.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, please contact the HamsterPi Developer Team at support@hamsterpi.com or via our Pi ecosystem developer channel.
